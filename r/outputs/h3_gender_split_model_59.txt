==================================================================================
4. VERDICT
==================================================================================

  Moderated mediation is NOT SUPPORTED - the bootstrap CI for the
  difference between conditional indirect effects includes zero.
  The TOTAL effect of c_oddity DOES differ by gender - ct3 CI excludes zero.

  Read this carefully. The TOTAL effect is moderated, but neither of its
  two components reaches significance on its own. That is not a
  contradiction: the total contrast is the sum of the direct and
  indirect contrasts, and a sum can be estimated more precisely than
  either part. It is also the pattern a marginal result produces when
  several tests are run without correction.
  Report the total effect difference as SUGGESTIVE, locate it in the
  direct pathway descriptively, and do not claim the mediation itself
  differs by gender.

  Pooled reference (h3_full_set.R): indirect = 1.1622, total = 0.7716.
  Here, indirect: Female = 1.1691, Male = 1.1412.
  Here, total:    Female = 0.8542, Male = 0.6763.

  Scale context for the total effect gap
    c_discom scale 1 to 4 (span 3), observed 1 to 4; SD = 1.0781
    Female   total effect 0.8542  = 28.47% of span = 0.792 SD
    Male     total effect 0.6763  = 22.54% of span = 0.627 SD
             gap          0.1779  = 5.93% of span = 0.165 SD

  Caveat: gender is measured, not manipulated. Even a significant
  contrast would be confounded with anything else differing between groups.
